# Themes

### Power & The State (Political Philosophy)

1. **The Social Contract:** How we justify being "ruled." 14 days from Hobbes’s "state of nature" to Rousseau’s "General Will" and Rawls’s "Veil of Ignorance."
2. **The Philosopher Kings:** A look at thinkers who held power—Marcus Aurelius, Confucius, and Julian the Apostate—and whether their theories survived the reality of the throne.
3. **Dissent & Disobedience:** The ethics of breaking the law. From Socrates’s refusal to flee his execution to Thoreau’s tax strike and Hannah Arendt’s analysis of civil disobedience.
4. **The Anatomy of Tyranny:** How republics turn into autocracies. Using Plato’s "Five Regimes," Machiavelli’s _The Prince_, Montesquieu’s warnings on the separation of powers, Hannah Arendt’s modern works, and Friedrich Hayek's theory on the tyranny of central planning.
5. **Property & Liberty:** Who "owns" the earth? Tracking the debate from John Locke’s "labor theory" to Proudhon’s "Property is Theft" and Hayek’s _Road to Serfdom_.
6. **The Individual vs. The Collective:** A clash of worldviews. 14 days comparing J.S. Mill’s _On Liberty_ with the collectivist theories of Marx and Hegel.
### Power & Governance

1. **The Rise and Fall of Empires:** Exploring the specific cultural "glue" that holds empires together and the tipping points that break them.
2. **The Great Schisms:** Exploring the moments when religions or ideologies split, and how those "divorces" still shape geopolitics.

### The Logic of the Universe (Philosophy of Science)

7. **The Death of Certainty:** How we know what is "true." From the Scholastic logic of the Middle Ages to Karl Popper’s "Falsification" and Thomas Kuhn’s "Paradigm Shifts."
8. **The Great Mechanics:** Is the world a machine? Exploring the "Clockwork Universe" through the eyes of Descartes, Newton, and Leibniz.
9. **The Darwinian Earthquake:** How evolution shattered philosophy. The 14-day fallout of 1859, touching on Nietzsche’s "God is Dead" and the rise of Pragmatism (William James).
10. **The Measurement Problem:** How physics broke philosophy. Exploring how Quantum Theory (Bohr vs. Einstein) forced us to rethink the nature of reality itself.

### The Ethics of Force (Philosophy of War)

11. **Just War Theory:** Can killing be moral? 14 days tracking the "rules of war" from St. Augustine and Aquinas to Michael Walzer’s modern interpretations.
12. **The Art of Strategy:** Eastern vs. Western military thought. Comparing Sun Tzu’s _Art of War_ (deception/efficiency) with the brutalist realism of Thucydides. Clausewitz, a deep dive into _On War_, exploring "Absolute War" vs. "Real War" and the relationship between the General and the Statesman.

### Historical Tipping Points (Thematic Synthesis)

13. **The Printing Press & The Truth:** How a piece of hardware broke the Catholic Church’s monopoly on "reality," leading to the Scientific Revolution.
14. **The Industrial Mind:** How factories changed the human soul. Analyzing the alienation of the worker through Marx, Smith, and the Luddite movement.
15. **Post-War Despair (Existentialism):** How the horrors of WWII gave birth to Sartre, Camus, and the search for meaning in a world that seemed to have none.
